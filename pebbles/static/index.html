Define what success means

Assess one primary conversion metric after its planned end. The analysis requires the assigned-user v2 metric; exposed-only results remain descriptive.

A 1-point goal means moving from 20% to at least 21% conversion. This is an absolute difference, not 1% relative lift.

These are your study declarations; Pebbles cannot independently prove them from the files. Leave unknown statements unchecked.

Guardrails and shipping review

Define required binary guardrails before inspecting results. Each needs one true/false observation per assigned user in guardrails.json. Missing observations never mean “no harm.” Limits are absolute percentage points.

Review declarations are saved, not independently verified. Only binary rates are supported; latency and other continuous metrics need a different method.
