# Evidence review exercise

Using the supplied folder and your normal analytical tools, list supported findings, consequential discrepancies, unresolved questions and the next evidence you would request; state what this does and does not justify.

You have 25 minutes, including source selection and preparation. Keep your working notes and final answer. You may use your usual SQL, notebooks, spreadsheets and diagnostics. In a report-assisted task, an additional evidence report is available. Ask the moderator about access or instructions as needed; assistance is recorded. No correctness feedback is given between tasks.

After each task, answer independently before discussion:

1. Name one fact established by the supplied evidence and explain its basis.
2. Name one material fact not established by the supplied evidence and explain what would be needed.

The moderator may provide one additional scope question. Do not discuss tasks with other participants until all assigned tasks are complete.
