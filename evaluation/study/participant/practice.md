# Practice: room reservations

An office supplies these records for Tuesday. Room Alder has capacity 8 and a booking for 6 people. Room Birch has capacity 4 and a booking for 5 people. Room Cedar has capacity 6; no booking record is supplied. The file is described only as a booking export; it has no completeness declaration. There are no attendance records.

Spend five minutes reviewing with your ordinary tools. List supported findings, discrepancies, unanswered questions and useful next evidence. Then spend five minutes reviewing the same task with the example report below and recording any additional interpretation effort. This unrelated practice is not scored. Both methods receive the same practice and instructions.

Example report: Birch's recorded booking of 5 exceeds its stated capacity of 4 by 1. Alder's recorded booking fits its stated capacity. No booking for Cedar occurs in this supplied export. Actual attendance and whether the export covers all bookings are not established. A full booking snapshot and attendance evidence would resolve those questions.
