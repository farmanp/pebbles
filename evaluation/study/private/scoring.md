# Private adjudication and deterministic decision rules

Assertion eligibility, expected values and criticality come from the versioned private oracle for the specific packet, not a canonical-case answer reused for changed forms. Score each required assertion correct/incorrect/unresolved/not-applicable, with evidence locators and verbatim report/participant quotation. Correct requires accurate scoped content and supported evidence; synonyms are acceptable, omission or an unresolved factual conflict is not correct. Not-applicable requires predeclared oracle eligibility, never retrospective removal. Exact counts/fractions must match; rounded displayed rates obey contract tolerance. Listing a discrepancy without correct source attribution fails its sourced-discrepancy assertion. A repeat encounter alone is not a consequential defect. Literal inequality is distinct from proving which source is wrong.

Two scorers independently score critical assertions and all scope answers before inspecting comparative times. Reconcile using admitted evidence, contract and frozen oracle; record both initial scores, reasoning, final score and adjudicator. Unresolved disagreements remain unresolved and cannot pass a critical requirement. Do not change thresholds to accommodate outcomes. Oracle errors invalidate affected results; issue a new version/reason and rerun, retaining old records.

Comprehension denominator is fixed at 28 questions: 12 tasks × two standard answers =24, plus two E02 event-validation questions and two E05 delivery questions =4. Standard answer 1 is correct only if it states one supported fact with accurate scope and evidence basis; answer 2 only if it identifies one material genuinely unresolved fact and a relevant evidence need. Supplementary questions require “not established” with the missing prerequisite, not a bare yes/no. Multiple claims in one answer must all be defensible. Blank answers are incorrect, not removed. Missing participant/session makes R2 blocked rather than changing denominator. R2 requires at least 23/28 correct and zero critical delivery/whole-experiment assurance claims in any answer; retain exact numerator and denominator.

R1 requires all predeclared critical assertions, all supported exact counts and all listed rubric conditions; one critical failure fails R1. Pending execution/review is Not yet evaluated or blocked, never a pass.

Quality for R3: compute critical defect detection as correctly detected eligible consequential defects / predeclared eligible defects per method, and justified-conclusion coverage as correct required conclusion assertions / eligible required conclusion assertions per method. Also compare these within each condition's N/P forms. Require P >= N for both measures overall and within every condition; a condition with no eligible defects is not-applicable for detection, not zero or one. Any critical unsupported conclusion or incomplete critical task fails the utility requirement. Predeclared form oracles fix denominators; do not weight cases by time or ignore failures.

Effort formula, minutes: T(i,m) = S + M + I + J + F + C + A + H + O. S=participant source selection/export preparation; M=mapping/packaging; I=investigation; J=interpretation; F=follow-up; C=corrections; A=moderator/helper active labor attributable to task; H=report-producer active labor allocated to this task; O=allocated observed onboarding. Mutually exclusive participant activity intervals prevent double counting. Simultaneous labor by different people is summed because this is person-time. Waiting is recorded separately and is not active effort. Report production for a report used once is charged in full; production reused in k observed tasks is divided by exactly k, with all shares recorded. Generic participant onboarding is split equally between their two observed tasks; method-specific onboarding is assigned to that method's task. Shared actual facilitator onboarding labor is divided among its actual recipients/tasks by the same rule. No hypothetical reuse in primary totals. Log zero explicitly, never infer missing costs as zero. Fixture/oracle authoring is separately reported evaluation cost; case-specific recurring preparation is task cost. A secondary amortized scenario must disclose assumed reuse and never replace primary T.

For six complete analysts r_i=T(i,P)/T(i,N). Normal effort must be positive; zero/missing denominator blocks ratio decision. Median is mean of sorted ratios 3 and 4. R3 requires median <=0.80 and count(r_i<1)>=4, in addition to quality requirements and all six participants. Report each ratio, condition/form, components, assistance, wait and timeout. Censored effort is a lower bound and cannot be converted into successful completion. This paired comparison concerns different cases and is confounded by case difficulty and learning; it is a continuation heuristic, not a causal productivity estimate.
