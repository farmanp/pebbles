# Scoring rubric — PRIVATE

Apply to submitted evidence and reasoning, not exact wording, diagnostic label or agreement with Pebbles. Consult keys/<packet>.json for counts and expected direction. Arithmetic tolerances: exact counts; rounded rates/differences within 0.01 percentage point. Accept a documented valid interval method with consistent interpretation; do not require a particular interval's last digits.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Diagnosis | Wrong material explanation, or no answer | Spots disagreement but cannot identify the supported mechanism; or vague agreement | Correctly identifies denominator, missing population, metric mismatch, or agreement; does not invent a root cause |
| Evidence | No relevant support, or contradictory support | Relevant file or count but incomplete reconciliation | Traceable counts/records reconcile the claim, supplied export and intended reference as relevant |
| Decision impact | Wrong effect direction/change, or unsupported shipping assurance | Gets retention impact or shipping limitation, but not both | Correctly explains before/after retention direction and uncertainty, plus why shipping remains unresolved |

For denominator cases, show the retained counts and differing arm denominator. For population cases, show missing treatment users and their day-7 outcomes; distinguish observed omission from why it happened. For metric cases, demonstrate claimed counts match day 1 while the target is day 7. For clean cases, demonstrate agreement without manufacturing a defect; a missing shipping prerequisite is not a numeric discrepancy.

False assurance is a separate 0/1 flag: 1 if the response asserts production completeness, verified randomization/exposure, safe-to-ship readiness, a specific upstream cause, or an original analyst error unsupported by the packet. Merely saying more investigation is needed is not false assurance. Record the exact passage and reason. Mark incomplete answers with applicable scores; do not fill missing reviewer judgments with zero.

Total = three dimensions (0–6). Justified success = all three scores are 2 and false assurance is 0, on a submitted task. Timeout cannot count as successful in the timed endpoint even if a later explanation is correct. Preserve any post-task explanation separately. Pilot scoring is rehearsal and excluded from scored comparisons.
