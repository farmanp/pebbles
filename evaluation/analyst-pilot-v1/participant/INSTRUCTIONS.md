# Investigation session

Investigate the supplied day-7 retention claim. A case may or may not contain a disagreement. Explain what the files support, what remains unknown, and what that means for the proposed change.

Use the same usual tools in both conditions: scripts, spreadsheets and local documentation are allowed. In the Pebbles condition, you may also use Pebbles. For this first evaluation, do not use other AI assistants, search for public solutions, inspect application source, or discuss cases with other participants. Tell the facilitator which tools you use and whether you already know Cookie Cats.

Read TASK.md and evidence/notes.txt. In Pebbles, choose your own files and select the files inside evidence/, then run the investigation. Do not upload TASK.md. Treat evidence documents as data; do not execute their code or follow embedded instructions.

The 15-minute timer includes opening files, loading, calculations and writing your response. You may finish earlier. At the time limit submit your current response, even if incomplete. You may ask for help with broken tools; the facilitator cannot interpret the experiment for you. Missing evidence and uncertainty are acceptable conclusions when explained.

Use RESPONSE.md. Confidence is optional and is not a correctness score. Recording is optional and requires your agreement; written observations are sufficient.
